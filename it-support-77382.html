Inside 77382

77382 is a standard street-delivery ZIP in Montgomery County, on the west side of The Woodlands among the ZIPs on our pages. USPS uses Spring as the preferred city and accepts The Woodlands. It is not part of the Town Center commercial core the way 77380 is; its business mix of professional and medical offices looks more like neighboring 77381, with comparatively lower employment.

The U.S. Census Bureau, 2023 ZIP Code Business Patterns recorded 746 employer establishments and 5,675 employees in 77382 (employment figures include Census noise infusion; noise flag G) — the lowest employment of the eight standard (non-PO Box) ZIP codes on our pages. The top sectors by establishment count were professional, scientific, and technical services (178), health care and social assistance (127), and finance and insurance (57).

Town Center, Research Forest, and Shenandoah City Hall are covered on the neighboring 77380 and 77381 pages.
