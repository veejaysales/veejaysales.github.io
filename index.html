How we work with you

A full-time IT staff rarely makes sense for a small business. A local partner who knows your systems, responds quickly, and stops problems before they spread usually does.

Fast answers, day or night

Support is available 24/7, with response times of 15-60 minutes, so a problem at 7 p.m. doesn't wait for the next business day.

Simple per-user pricing

Routine support doesn't come with surprise invoices. Managed plans are a flat monthly rate per user, starting at $70 per user per month, on a 12-month agreement with a $1,000 monthly minimum. Before you sign anything, we'll go over what you actually need.

Example: 15 users × $70 = $1,050 per month. Teams of 14 or fewer pay the $1,000 minimum.

Headquartered in The Woodlands

We're based in The Woodlands, work with businesses across the Houston, The Woodlands, and San Antonio areas, and are members of the Schertz Chamber of Commerce. Call, text, or email whenever you need us — 24/7, not just business hours.

Problems caught early

We monitor your systems in the background and send regular updates on the health of your technology. If something looks off, you'll hear from us, and we'll fix it.

Built around your workflow
