Spring at a glance

Spring has no city hall. It is an unincorporated community and Census-designated place in Harris County, with no municipal government of its own. The Houston Northwest Chamber describes the wider Champions–1960–Spring–Northwest Harris County area the same way — unincorporated, with no town hall, mayor, or main street — and places it roughly 20 miles from Houston's central business district.

The postal map is bigger than the community. Spring mail addresses extend into Montgomery County, and USPS lists Spring as the preferred city on every ZIP code covered by our local pages.

Business activity follows I-45 North, the main commercial route through the area. The Spring Post Office sits at 1411 Wunsche Loop in 77373. Old Town Spring, the historic shopping and dining district, has its own state-created Old Town Spring Improvement District under Chapter 3813 of the Texas Special District Local Laws Code.

Just north, Oak Ridge North is a small incorporated city whose name USPS accepts on 77386. Its City Hall, at 27424 Robinson Road, Oak Ridge North, TX 77385, falls outside the ZIP codes on these pages — and because 77386 covers a large area, not all of it is Oak Ridge North.
